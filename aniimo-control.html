✏️ Edit the list

📷 Form photos: every form has a 📷 - click it and paste a snip of the form you just caught or hatched (it ticks the form too). You can also drop a photo straight onto a form.
🖼️ Pictures: drag a picture onto any card, or open ✏️ and paste a snip (Win+Shift+S, then Ctrl+V). You can also drop files into ICB\assets\aniimo named by number or name (e.g. 001.png or Emberpup.png).
To change one Aniimo, use the ✏️ on its card. The starting list came from your friend's tracker (community info - the game may differ).
