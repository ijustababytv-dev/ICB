➕ Add a widget

Tip: you can paste any widget link here, like the NeoStream one or a StreamElements/Streamlabs link.

❓ First time

  1. Double-click start-desktop-widgets.bat in your ICB folder. The first time, it downloads what it needs (a minute or two).
  2. A pink icon appears in your system tray (bottom right). The status at the top of this page turns green.
  3. Set your game to Borderless Windowed. Widgets can't sit on top of a true fullscreen game.
