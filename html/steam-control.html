1 · Connect to Steam

  1. Get your free key: open steamcommunity.com/dev/apikey, sign in, type localhost as the domain, tick the box, press Register, and copy the Key.
  2. Copy your Steam profile link (Steam → click your name → Profile → right-click → Copy Page URL).
  3. Make achievements visible: Steam profile → Edit Profile → Privacy Settings → Game details: Public.
4126040 = Aniimo (other Steam games work too)
