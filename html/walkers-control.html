Each unicorn switches between the ones that are on every 10–20 seconds, so they don't all move the same way. Ghost pictures always float.

Who gets a unicorn

ChatterUnicorn picture
With "Everyone", anyone who chats is ticked automatically — untick someone to leave them out. With "Only subscribers", subs are ticked automatically (anyone with sub months in the giveaway — live subs, or ones you added as "already subbed") and you can tick anyone else. "Reset" puts someone back to automatic. Bots and names on your giveaway block list never get one. The picture menu shows ⭐ subscriber-only pictures for subs only. 📷 Their own uploads a picture of their choice just for that person — nobody else ever gets it, and it's never used at random (saved in ICB\assets\decor\unicorn-personal\).
