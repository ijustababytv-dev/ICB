📰 News Ticker

A scrolling news bar along the bottom of your stream. Change it any time — it updates live.

Add as a Meld browser layer. Make it full-screen (the bar sits at the very bottom) or a thin strip across the bottom.

Messages

One message at a time: each one scrolls all the way off the screen before the next ticked message comes in, over and over. Untick one to skip it without deleting it.

⚠️ Quick alert

An alert takes over the whole bar (flashing, "⚠️ ALERT" label), then your normal messages come back by themselves.
