Chat

You plus your mods' names, separated by commas. Meld doesn't tell ICB who is a mod, so list them here. Nobody else can trigger it.
Turn on if sprite names clash with other commands (like !crown or !honey).

Where the sprites are

Sprites without sticker art yet show once, with their normal picture.
On: they go away as you fill the book. Off: everything stays, and caught ones glow gold.
For sprites shown once: gone when every sticker is collected.
Floor — the strip along the bottom of the screen where the toys lie around.
Off: toys go all the way to the left edge and the book sits on top of them when it's open.
Every toy keeps its own spot, and a few are tipped over. Now and then one wiggles.

Inside of the jar (where sprites can go)

Base sprites

The first sticker of each sprite (Base) gets its own size and its own catch moment.
The Base sticker has the left page to itself; the variants are on the right page under My collection. 100% = same size as a variant.
Catching a Base shows "✨ New sprite: Jonesy! ✨" with an extra-big sparkle burst.

Sticker art

Put each sprite's art in assets/sprites/<Sprite Name>/ named like Jonesy.png (Base), Jonesy_Gold.png, Jonesy_Loot_Hacker.png. New files are picked up by themselves — no restart.

Sticker book

Chat (you + your list): !open · !close
On: when it's closed, the book swings open for the catch, then closes again. Off: the toy flies into the closed book.
Chat (you + your list): !page next · !page back · !page jonesy
0 = off. Otherwise it turns to the next page every so many seconds when nothing is happening.
